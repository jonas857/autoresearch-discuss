# autoresearch-discuss

A research workspace analyzing the human-AI interaction mechanism of `/autoresearch`.

## Core framework

**Stateful Value-Signal Research Loop**: a long-running AI interaction pattern where humans provide value signals and governance, AI maintains artifact-mediated state, inner loops produce evidence, outer loops produce meaning, and progress reports close the human steering loop.

## Hypotheses

- H1: Stateful artifacts are the core interaction medium.
- H2: Human-AI collaboration is asymmetric producer-consumer exchange.
- H3: Dual-loop architecture separates production of evidence from production of meaning.
- H4: Progress reports are the primary governance interface.

All four hypotheses are currently supported by conceptual analysis and artifact/loop/governance mapping.
