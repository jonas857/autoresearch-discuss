# H2 Protocol: Human-AI Collaboration as Producer-Consumer Exchange

## Hypothesis

The most useful interaction model for `/autoresearch` is an asymmetric producer-consumer exchange: humans produce goals, values, constraints, and feedback; AI produces structured research labor, synthesis, and reports.

## Mechanism

Open-ended research contains too much low-level uncertainty for humans to productively micromanage every step. The human's scarce contribution is judgment and value-setting. The AI's scalable contribution is persistent execution and information compression.

## Prediction

A first-principles decomposition should show that high-quality use of `/autoresearch` depends less on detailed human step-by-step commands and more on clear human specification of:

- objective;
- value function;
- constraints;
- audience;
- feedback cadence.

## Evaluation Method

Analyze the workflow from both perspectives:

1. Human as producer.
2. AI as consumer.
3. AI as producer.
4. Human as consumer.
5. File artifacts as intermediate market / exchange layer.

## Confirmatory Criteria

H2 is supported if the model explains both good usage patterns and failure modes, especially over-control by humans and under-specification by humans.

## Status

Initially supported by prior conceptual analysis. Further validation should compare against alternative models such as command-execution, delegation-only, or human-in-the-loop approval models.
